Why antacids aren't enough

Antacids: quick relief, then the acid comes back once it wears off. Ginger or mint water: sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't. Medicine only when it hurts: your stomach isn't cared for every day.

Why acid keeps rising

When the valve at the end of your oesophagus is weak, stomach acid rises up. Heavy, spicy or oily meals, kopi, late suppers and lying down soon after eating all make it worse.

What it does to your oesophagus

Acid that keeps rising wears away the oesophagus wall bit by bit. You can't see it, but the damage happens every time the burning comes. Left for years, the wall can become wounded, swollen and scarred.

WARNING

Don't wait until your oesophagus starts to get damaged.
