Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Do I mix it with water?

Yes. Pour 1 sachet into 150–200 ml of warm water, stir well and drink right away.

When is the best time to drink it?

Once a day, before or after meals. What matters most is taking it every day, consistently.

How long should I take it?

The process takes 2 weeks to 6 months, depending on the person. The key is consistency: 1 sachet a day, no breaks.

What does it taste like?

Tasty and never gets boring, say our own customers. GERTRIVA has no cow's milk, so it suits people who are lactose intolerant.

I'm on doctor's medicine. Can I take it?

GERTRIVA is a food product, not medicine. Keep taking the medicine your doctor gave you. If unsure, ask your doctor or pharmacist first.

Pregnant, breastfeeding or allergic to soy?

Get your doctor's advice first if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or have a chronic illness. GERTRIVA contains soy, so avoid it if you are allergic to soy.

Do you deliver all over Singapore?

Yes. We deliver islandwide in Singapore for free, and you pay cash (COD) when your order arrives.

Orders are processed on working days and delivered islandwide in Singapore. For COD, you pay cash to the courier when your order arrives. Delivery is free.
